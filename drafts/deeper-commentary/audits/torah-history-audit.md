# Torah and historical books: deeper expansion audit

Status: reviewable research draft only. No production data, code, commits or deployment changed.

## Scope and method

All 21 assigned books (Genesis–2 Maccabees), all 340 existing note bodies, their anchors/editorial coverage, and the existing linked explanations touching these books were reviewed. Full source sections were read for every new item; Bible anchors and surrounding narrative were read from the current WEB dataset. Truncated inspection outputs were revisited in smaller batches. This is a selective thematic expansion, not a claim that every unannotated verse has been exhaustively surveyed or every existing source reverified.

The retained group contains **16 notes and 8 linked explanations**. A further sacred-music connection is owned by the letters group after merging overlapping drafts. There is no minimum per-book allocation: several already densely annotated books receive no new item. Six drafted local notes were removed after comparison: Genesis hospitality and envy, Exodus craft, Gideon’s ephod, Chronicles music, and Rizpah’s vigil. The music material was merged into a connection owned by the letters group; the others were omitted, as detailed below.

## Strongest additions

- Aaron’s bearing of Israel’s names on shoulders and heart makes priestly representation concrete. The comparison with Christ’s intercession is expressly Christian contemplation, not an asserted quotation by Hebrews.
- Judah’s changed willingness to bear another brother’s cost is repentance visible in a changed role; the separate Lion/Lamb connection reads royal strength through Augustine’s account of Christ’s voluntary self-gift.
- Zelophehad’s daughters are heard within their actual ancient inheritance system; neither the improvement nor the continuing limits are hidden.
- Chronicles 28 and the Samaritan display striking practical parallels (clothing/food/oil/donkeys/Jericho) while the connection explicitly avoids claiming a demonstrated literary dependency.
- Asa and Sirach read together prevent an isolated passage from being used against medical care.
- Ezra and Paul are not flattened into one instruction; Catholic marriage teaching supplies the Christian frame for mixed-belief households.

## Sources and interpretive limits

New note citations:15 Catechism citations and 1 patristic citation (Chrysostom 1 Corinthians 32.13). Connections cite seven Catechism pages and AugustineCityofGod 16.41. Supporting excerpts were checked against fetched text; source-cache raw-HTML hashes verified. The Fathers’ individual readings are identified as such. Modern applications are presented as applications, rather than invented ancient claims. No new Hebrew or Greek assertion was made without evidence. No mystical quotation was improvised: the parent agent handles verified CCEL primary works and any shared mystical additions.

This batch deliberately uses older primary Christian readings without importing their dated stereotypes or treating each allegorical detail as binding doctrine. Genesis 49’s lion reading is selective and explicit; it does not transfer Augustine’s neighbouring supersessionist discussion into the commentary. The Moses/Elijah link is based on the explicit synthesis in CCC 2583 and remains distinct from the existing general Transfiguration witness connection.
