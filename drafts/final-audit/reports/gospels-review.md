## Findings

No substantive correction to the prose was required by this review. Shortening generally removes repeated explanation while retaining the interpretive point. In particular, the revisions retain real humanity and divinity, bodily resurrection, sacramental efficacy and communal consequences, repentance without despair, prayer without guaranteed physical recovery, protection compatible with forgiveness, and the distinction between the Gospel text and a named author's spiritual application. The added notes appropriately resist collective Jewish guilt, confusing illness with possession, reading Jesus' brothers as a denial of Catholic teaching, and treating protective signs as permission for reckless tests of God.

Significance is coherent: central doctrine and corrections to consequential misreadings are fundamental; narrower devotional practices and theological applications are interest. The Jesus Prayer and Augustine's coin/image application are appropriately interest, while the joined love commandments and prophetic Temple quotations are fundamental. No unsupported Greek etymology or historical assertion was identified. The John 21 note carefully avoids turning the variation in love words into an obligatory spiritual hierarchy; Augustine explicitly treats love and liking together in the cited passage.

One evidence-quality issue required correction. Several records contained only the first roughly 30 words of a CCC paragraph, sometimes stopping mid-sentence before the claim they purported to support. All seven CCC-based new notes initially exhibited this pattern; consequential existing examples included notes 734, 748, 834, 1133, 1134 and 1138, and connections concerning Mary as ark, Gethsemane's answer, continued illness, forgiveness and memory, and Jesus' human knowing. Reading the full paragraphs confirmed the prose, but the stored evidence needed decisive exact sentences. This was reported to the author and root. The author replaced these excerpts with short supporting sentences, preserving the prose and source URLs. The targeted corrections were independently re-read and accepted. The author also reports extending this correction across all 174 unique CCC source paragraphs in the Gospel file; that broader sweep is the author's coverage, not an independent rereading claim by this reviewer.

Duplicate visible links to the same CCC page were also identified (including notes 695, 698 and 744). The author consolidated the note display links while retaining each structured source record. This is a presentation correction, not a change in evidential scope.
