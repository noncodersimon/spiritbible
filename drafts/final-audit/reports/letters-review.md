## Findings resolved during review

1. Note 953 could imply that chastity finds fulfilment only in marriage. It now first defines chastity generally, then separately explains its form within marriage.
2. Note 962's discussion of the saints' intercession had only a general Paschal-participation source. Added CCC 956 supplies direct support for the saints' prayer through Christ the one mediator; CCC 618 remains for participation in his unique saving work.
3. Note 1004's statement that anointing does not guarantee immediate recovery now has direct CCC 1508 support alongside the sacrament's recognition in CCC 1510.
4. Numerous evidence excerpts initially stopped before the sentence supporting the relevant claim. The author replaced targeted excerpts and performed a wider sweep. Final checks confirmed, in particular, Chalcedon's actual confession rather than the heresy rejected in CCC 467 (985), the manna/Eucharist sentence in CCC 1094 (1037), the river/Spirit identification in CCC 1137 (1053), and Chrysostom's actual explanation of the manna quotation. Earlier repairs included original sin, Israel's irrevocable calling, grace and freedom, sacramental discipline, the undivided soul, descent to the dead and final self-exclusion. These were provenance improvements; the prose already had support in the full cited contexts.
