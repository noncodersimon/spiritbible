The Leviticus 12 distinction is narrowly supported by the short encyclopedia entry and actual ritual context. Leviticus 20 distinguishes the ancient penalty, present moral teaching about acts, and personal dignity without suppressing any of them. The firstfruits, heavenly sanctuary and Aaron/Wisdom links are grounded in their specific passages, not generic thematic association.
