# Catechism thematic audit

## Method and scope

Read all 80 “In Brief” sections across the Catechism's four parts, then read the complete relevant sections for the proposed Eucharistic, Trinitarian, prayer and moral clarifications. The source catalogue contains all374 section pages from the Vatican archive. Cached availability is not itself a claim to have read every paragraph. Book editors separately read the biblical chapters, existing commentary and supporting source contexts; their checklists document that work.

The existing1162 notes already covered the central doctrinal structure well. The main improvement is greater precision and stronger connections, rather than adding one note for every Catechism heading. The table names representative coverage and the refinements selected during this audit. It is a qualitative map, not an assertion that these are the only relevant passages.

## Editorial boundaries

No attempt to make the annotations a complete systematic theology or to settle every scholarly dispute. A literary echo, an individual Father's interpretation and a doctrinal teaching are identified according to what the sources warrant. “Fundamental” and “of interest” describe importance for reading the passage, not competing grades of truth. The approved sources remain the Catechism, NewAdvent's Fathers/Encyclopedia and the three existing CCEL spiritual works. No new papal documents were used.

All73 existing book introductions were separately read by the root editor in this audit. They remain useful, accurate guides to literary setting and Catholic reception; no substantive rewrite was warranted. The Bible wording itself remains matched to the pinned publisher files.
