The new connection, **`papal-mercy-giver-and-receiver`**, joins Matthew 5:7 and 25:34–40. Its point is not merely that love received should be passed on, which is already covered. John Paul II identifies a reciprocity within the actual act of mercy: the giver also receives through the person helped. It therefore challenges a superior donor/inferior recipient model. The wording explicitly excludes a bargain or a demand for gratitude. Paragraph 14 itself and its footnotes supply both endpoints.
